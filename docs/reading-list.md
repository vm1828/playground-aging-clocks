# Reading list

Source papers behind each tool, clock, or dataset — read up-front for a
reading-first curriculum, or come back to them when stuck.
Build-first alternative: [learning-plan.md](learning-plan.md).

---

## Applying Horvath 2013

**1. Horvath & Raj 2018 — best entry point.** A review by Horvath himself
covering the 2013 clock + later clocks (Hannum, PhenoAge, GrimAge) in plain
language. Best on-ramp to field vocabulary.

> _Nature Reviews Genetics_ 19, 371–384 (2018).
> <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41576-018-0004-3>

**2. Horvath 2013 — the source of the 353 numbers.** Focus on the intro,
the "DNA methylation age estimator" section (how the 353 CpGs were selected
via elastic-net), and the age transformation formula (we use it literally).
Skim the per-tissue validation tables — keep the headline (works across 51
tissues), skip memorising numbers.

> _Genome Biology_ 14, R115 (2013).
> <https://genomebiology.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/gb-2013-14-10-r115>

**3. Dataset source papers.** Read intro + experimental procedures for any
methylation dataset you use.

- **GSE36054** → Alisch RS, et al. _Genome Research_ 22, 623–632 (2012).
- **GSE40279** → Hannum G, et al. _Molecular Cell_ 49, 359–367 (2013).
